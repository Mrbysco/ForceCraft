## Big rewrite
**ForceCraft has been rewritten straight to 26.1.2**

With that missing features will be re-added later on a per-feature basis.

List of features that weren't in the 1.21.1 version:
* Force Planks can now be made into more blocks like buttons, signs, hanging signs, doors, trapdoors, fences, fence gates, pressure plates and even Shelves
* Force Bricks can now have Stairs, Slab and Wall variants
* Bombs and Super Bombs have been added
* Force Bows infused with Freezing will now convert blocks like water into ice (Conversions can be added onto through datapacks)
* Rod of Vision has been implemented (that grants night-vision)
* Rod of Invisibility has been implemented (that grants invisibility)
* Rod of Speed (up to 5) has been implemented (that grants increased movement speed, jump boost and mining efficiency)
* Rod of Time now has 4 modes: Stop, Slower, Fast, Hyper
* Time Torch also has the same 4 modes and can be changed per right click
* Roc's Feather has been added
* The Force Engine now looks more like the old Force Engine with the buildcraft style animation
